A few helpful details.

Where does JC Java serve?

We serve events in Eastern Carolina. Travel 50 miles outside Pitt County, NC may carry a mileage charge. Call with your venue details to confirm availability.

Can we customize a package?

Yes. Ask about additional service hours, drink options, or a menu tailored to your event.

Are there options for guests who don’t drink coffee?

Our published packages include non-coffee choices, with options varying by package. We also offer tea. Tell us what your guests would enjoy when planning your event.

How much setup time should we allow?

Please allow 1–2 hours for setup and breakdown, in addition to your selected service time. Confirm venue access and timing with our team.
